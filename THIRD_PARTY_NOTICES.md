Realmwise is distributed with or depends on open-source packages, platform
components, and services. Each dependency retains its own copyright, license,
and notice requirements. This file is a guide, not a replacement for the
license text shipped by a dependency; consult `pubspec.yaml`, `pubspec.lock`,
and the dependency distribution for the applicable notices. We do not attempt
to reproduce every dependency license here.

The App can request public metadata from OpenLibrary and BoardGameGeek/RPGGeek
and can sync through Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, or Dropbox. Their APIs,
terms, privacy policies, service marks, and returned data are controlled by
their respective providers. API responses and external data are not relicensed
by this project, and provider names and marks are used only to identify
compatibility.

Before redistributing builds, confirm the provenance and license of every
included font, icon, image, sample, and other asset, and confirm any required
trademark or attribution rights. When in doubt, retain the upstream notice or
seek permission from the rights holder.
