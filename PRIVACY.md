# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2026-08-15**

This policy describes the privacy practices for Realmwise (the "App"), maintained by The Grognard Codes / Jacob Davis.

## Information the App handles

Realmwise is local-first. Your catalog, notes, and images are stored on your device. The Grognard Codes and Jacob Davis do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your personal information to themselves or to other third parties.

The App makes network requests only when you initiate a feature that needs them:

- **OpenLibrary and BoardGameGeek/RPGGeek:** search and enrichment requests send the query or identifiers you submit and receive public metadata. Requests are not made in the background.
- **Cloud sync:** if you configure sync, the App authenticates with the provider you select (Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, or Dropbox) and synchronizes a catalog backup bundle for the active device. This is a single-device backup and controlled handoff mechanism, not real-time multi-device merging. The provider copy may include lease/handoff metadata (such as device identity, account identity, and timestamps) needed to coordinate ownership; it does not include information collected by The Grognard Codes beyond what is needed for sync. Provider terms and privacy policies apply to that account and service.

Authentication tokens and provider settings are kept in the platform’s secure storage where available. Disconnecting removes local credentials; it does not necessarily delete a remote sync bundle, so use the provider’s controls if you want to remove that copy.

If a device was offline while it was no longer the active sync owner, its local edits are not automatically merged into the current bundle. Review and transfer those changes deliberately before deleting or restoring local data. On Android, provider sign-in uses the provider's Android-compatible OAuth/browser flow; desktop loopback details do not apply.

## Your choices and control

You can use the catalog offline, decline remote lookups, disconnect a sync provider, delete local databases and images, and remove remote sync data through the selected provider. The App has no advertising, analytics, or account operated by The Grognard Codes.

## Children and changes

The App is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy when the App changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer through the repository or distribution channel where you obtained the App.
